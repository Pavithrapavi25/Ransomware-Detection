SYSTEM FAQ

01

How does the Prediction engine work?

Our system employs image-based malware Prediction. Executables are converted to grayscale binary images, then analyzed by a convolutional neural network trained on extensive ransomware datasets. This approach identifies structural patterns that signature-based methods cannot detect.

02

What file formats are supported?

The system accepts grayscale images derived from Windows PE executables. Standard image uploads will not yield accurate results. For optimal Prediction, use images generated from binary executables using proper conversion tools.

03

How is my data protected?

Zero-trust architecture ensures complete privacy. All files are processed in volatile memory and immediately purged after analysis. No data is logged, stored, or transmitted to external systems. Your files remain entirely under your control.
